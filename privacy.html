1. Information we collect

Photos and images. Roomify processes photos that you capture or upload for enhancement — property interiors and exteriors, listing photographs, and associated visual content.

Listing information. Property addresses, listing names, listing metadata, and user-generated notes.

3. Artificial intelligence processing

When you submit a photo for enhancement, the photo is transmitted to OpenAI’s API for processing. OpenAI receives the image and returns an enhanced version to Roomify. As of the effective date of this Privacy Policy:

  • OpenAI API submissions are not used to train OpenAI models.
  • Roomify does not use your uploaded photos to train artificial intelligence models.
  • Roomify does not sell or license your photos to third parties.

Users should review OpenAI’s privacy and data-handling policies for additional information.

We may share information with trusted service providers that help us operate the Services:
