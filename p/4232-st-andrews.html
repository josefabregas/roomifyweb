The home

A Mediterranean estate, reimagined.

Tucked into one of Las Colinas' most coveted streets, 4232 St Andrews Boulevard combines the proportions of a custom Mediterranean estate with the warmth of a true family home. Light pours in through tall windows; oak floors run uninterrupted from the great room to the chef's kitchen.

Designed for both formal entertaining and quiet evenings, the home features four spacious bedrooms, four-and-a-half baths, and thoughtfully detailed interiors throughout — from the hand-finished cabinetry to the spa-styled primary suite.
